Summary: PuzzLink PH is a free word puzzle game that rewards players with real GCash/Maya cash. We collect only the data we need to run the game, pay you, and prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Who We Are

PuzzLink PH ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by [Your Business Name / Your Name], based in the Philippines. For privacy inquiries, contact us at: [your-email@domain.com]

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

a) Device Information

  • Android ID (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID) — used to create and restore your game account across reinstalls
  • Device model and Android OS version — used for performance optimization and anti-fraud device fingerprinting
  • IP address — used for fraud detection (e.g., detecting non-Philippine traffic on accounts claiming PH payouts)

b) Account Information

  • Username (if you create one)
  • GCash or Maya mobile number — only collected when you submit a payout request, and only used to process your disbursement
  • Account holder name — required by GCash/Maya for payment compliance

c) Gameplay & Transaction Data

  • Puzzle levels completed and time taken — used to calculate rewards and detect automated bots
  • Coin balance and transaction history — maintained server-side for accuracy and fraud prevention
  • Ad impression IDs — provided by ad networks to prevent duplicate reward claims

d) Advertising Identifiers

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) — used by our ad partners (Google AdMob, AppLovin) to serve relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your Android Settings → Google → Ads.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your game account
  • To calculate and credit coin rewards for puzzle completions and ad views
  • To process GCash/Maya cash disbursements when you request a payout
  • To detect and prevent fraud, multi-account abuse, and bot activity
  • To serve advertisements through our ad partners
  • To improve game performance and fix bugs

5. Payment Processing

Cash disbursements are processed through Xendit, a licensed Philippine payment service provider regulated by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP). Your GCash/Maya number and account name are transmitted to Xendit for the sole purpose of completing your payout. Xendit's privacy policy: xendit.co/privacy-policy

6. Data Retention

  • Transaction records are retained for 7 years for financial compliance
  • Payout records are retained for 7 years per BSP requirements
  • Device fingerprints and risk flags are retained for 2 years
  • If you request account deletion, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law
